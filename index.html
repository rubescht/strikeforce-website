GRID REF · AI-SEC / DOME SHIELD
Primary mission

The new front line is your AI

Your staff are already using AI. Every prompt can carry client data, credentials or IP, and attackers now talk their way past defences instead of breaking in. Dome Shield, our AI security platform, sits between your people and every AI tool to see, control and prove what happens.

HOSTILE 01

Data leakage

Client records, contracts and passwords pasted into public chatbots can end up outside your control for good.

HOSTILE 02

Prompt injection

Crafted instructions hidden in emails, files or web pages trick AI into revealing data or taking actions.

HOSTILE 03

Shadow AI

Unapproved apps and browser plug-ins nobody has assessed, quietly connected to your files.

HOSTILE 04

Agent hijacking

AI agents with access to inboxes and systems can be steered to send, delete or approve on an attacker's behalf.

HOSTILE 05

Over-trust

Confident but wrong answers drive legal, financial and reputational mistakes when nobody checks the output.

    Field questions

    Straight answers

    We're a small business. Are we really a target?

    Yes. Small and medium businesses are targeted because they hold valuable data and usually have fewer defences. Medium-sized businesses saw the sharpest rise in losses in the latest ASD report. Our tiers are sized so smaller teams get the same 24/7 cover as large ones.

    Do we have to ban ChatGPT or Copilot to stay safe?

    No. Bans push staff to use AI on personal devices where you can't see anything. Dome Shield lets your people keep using the AI tools that help them, while sensitive data is redacted or blocked and every interaction is logged.

    What happens when you detect an attack at 2am?

    Our analysts investigate and contain it straight away, for example by isolating a device or disabling a compromised account, then call your nominated contact. You get a clear incident report in the morning, not a pile of alerts.

    Will this help with Essential Eight, ISO 27001 and insurance?

    Yes. We map controls to Essential Eight, ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 for AI, and produce continuous evidence you can hand to auditors, insurers and your board.

    We already have an IT provider. Do we need to switch?

    No. We work alongside your existing IT team or provider. If you'd like one team for both, our sister company Midnight IT delivers 24/7 managed IT within the same group.

    How quickly can you get us protected?

    The free Recon assessment comes first. Once you choose a level of deployment we agree a rollout plan and timeline with you upfront, with the highest-risk gaps closed first.
